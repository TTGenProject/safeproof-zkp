SafeProof: Privacy-Preserving Harassment Reporting with Threshold Zero-Knowledge Disclosure

Le Huyen Trang (Hazel)

TTGen Project
ACM womENcourage™ 2026 Hackathon, Sophia Antipolis, France

Abstract

SafeProof is a reporting protocol for workplace sexual harassment and for online harassment and stalking. A reporter can show that a pattern of abuse exists and that they are a distinct, real, independent reporter, without revealing their identity or their evidence until they choose to. It combines a tamper-evident ledger of salted evidence commitments, a nullifier scheme that allows one report per person per target, and a threshold zero-knowledge proof that reveals only how many distinct people have reported one target.

Keywords: zero-knowledge proofs, nullifiers, Sybil resistance, oblivious PRF, harassment reporting, privacy, digital well-being.

Research prototype. SafeProof is not a support service, a legal instrument, or a substitute for trauma-informed care.
