Reporting harassment is risky because the reporter pays first. They give their name before anyone has decided anything, and they can't find out whether others have reported the same person until they have taken that risk themselves.

**SafeProof** is a reporting protocol for workplace sexual harassment and for online harassment and stalking. It lets a reporter show two things: **that a pattern of abuse exists**, and **that they are a distinct, real, independent reporter**. They reveal neither their identity nor their evidence until they choose to. The protocol combines three parts. A tamper-evident ledger stores salted commitments to evidence. A nullifier scheme allows one report per person per target. A threshold zero-knowledge proof tells an intermediary *how many* distinct people have reported one target, and nothing about who they are.

**Keywords:** zero-knowledge proofs, nullifiers, Sybil resistance, oblivious PRF, harassment reporting, privacy, digital well-being.
