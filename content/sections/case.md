In an AP video report, an American woman who was interning in Vietnam describes, in her own words, reporting a rape to the police. Vietnam has since charged a man with the assault, but she says the police asked her to reenact it. Her account shows the barriers from the next section in a single case: the reporter pays first, she has to give her name and relive the harm before anyone has acted, and each new step of the process asks her for the whole account again. SafeProof does not replace an investigation. It aims to change the order: a reporter is counted first, without exposing themselves or their evidence, and a pattern of reports can become visible before any single person has to carry that cost alone.
